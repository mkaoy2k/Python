Compassionate and Great Buddha！
A new life has been born！

We pray that under your blessings, the child will be free from calamities and hardships, healthy in body and mind； may the child be blessed day and night, and grow up peacefully.

Please grant the child the fruits of happiness, but also give him a few setbacks and trials；
Please grant the child a garden of joy, but also give him a few thorns and weeds.
Please let the child learn to be grateful for the contributions of others, possess the virtue of giving back, and not be blinded by vanity or misled by sensual pleasures.

When the child is frustrated, please give him confidence and courage, and do not let him be sad or sigh.
When the child is in distress, please give him wisdom and strength, and do not let him be sorrowful or disappointed.
This heartfelt wish may extend to The Three Thousand Great Thousand Worlds； this vow may reach the boundless future.

This young life！
We must not let him lose his innocent nature.
This young life！
We must not let him lose his kind and beautiful feelings.

Compassionate and great Buddha！
Please accept my sincere prayers.
